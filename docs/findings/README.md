# Findings

A finding is one answered question, in one short file: what was asked, what was measured, what came out, what it rules out and what it doesn't cover. One is written the day a gate passes or fails, or a question is settled, and it is not edited afterwards except to change its status; a later result gets its own number. The long stage notes in `../notes/` stay the chronicle of every run; these are what we know.

## Statuses

- `confirmed`: passed on a fresh block of seeds never used for choosing.
- `held-out`: held-out seeds only.
- `hint`: one seed, a small sample, or a first look.
- `open`: the question is stated, not settled.
- `ruled out`: a reading the evidence rejects.

## Shape

Line 1 is `# NNNN Title`, line 3 is `date: … · status: … · seeds: … · brains: …`, then these bold labels in order: Question, World and brains, Method, Result (one table, numbers copied from the notes), What it rules out, Limits, Replays and clips, Links. Files are `NNNN-short-slug.md`.
