**Question.** The fresh brain's grass line reached standing to graze by a ladder of one-change rungs (0031). The colony brain's line, carried through the colony stages, went up the same reward rungs (needs_lost 6, hunger) and then collapsed at the first grass rung, and two skip-ahead probes collapsed the same way. Is the colony brain missing something the ladder can't give it, or is something in its training in the way?

**World and brains.** 0029's grazing world: one nim, 90 s games, two meadows of 1.2 m radius, grass the only food. g1e-c29 is the colony brain after the two reward rungs, in the world where a bite at speed v is worth `clip(1 − v/4)` (still 4). From it, 6M each:
- g1f-c29: still 4 → 2, as the fresh line's 1f.
- g1f3-c29: a half step, still 4 → 3.
- g1skip2-c29: straight to the fresh line's rung 1i world (still 0.5, turning costs energy); g1skip-c29 jumped there from before the reward rungs.
- g1f-c29-spread (two seeds): rung 1f from a copy of g1e-c29's checkpoint whose thrust and turn spread (the learned `log_std`, which has no floor) is set to 0.35, nothing else changed.
- g1e-c29-spread-g4: the same copy trained in g1e's own world (still 4), the control for the reset alone.
- The pond brain's line (0028's sc2 brain, carried onto the grass): g1h-turn-sc2 is its rung 1h (turning costs energy, still 1) from g1g-sc2; g1h-sc2-turnspread is the same rung, same seed (938; and 948 for a second), from a copy of g1g-sc2 with only the turn spread reset to 0.35 (its thrust spread was 0.45).

**Method.** Frozen probes over 64 sampled games, last checkpoints: `anchor_probe.py` (a moment is anchored when the nim stays within 0.25 m of where it stood for the next 3 s, in a meadow; as in 0031) and `grass_probe_v2.py` (speed in a meadow, deaths). The spread is read from each checkpoint.

For scale, the fresh brain's line has spreads of 0.26 to 0.52 at every rung, and its rung 1f (still 2) ran slow loops at 1.14 m/s, anchored 0.1%. The plain g1f-c29 run's games last about 25 s from its first update to its last.

**What it rules out.** That the colony brain needs the whole ladder, or can't stand: with its spread reset it stands at rung 1f, 94 to 95% of its meadow time on two seeds, with stands of 22 to 30 s, longer than any rung of the fresh line. That a price on turning is enough to stop a brain whose turning has no randomness left: the pond brain's line turned at the clip on every choice with the price, and on about 2% once its turn spread was reset, standing 84 and 86% of its meadow time on two seeds. That a gentler world step is enough: at still 3 it lives, but still races at full speed, and its spread stays at 0.03. What happened instead: after its long training the colony brain's steering had almost no randomness left (0.03, against about 0.35 for a fresh brain), so when racing stopped paying it never tried going slower, every nim starved from the first update, and nothing it did gave the learning a way out. The reset alone slows it right down and stands it half the time even where a fast bite still pays (51% at still 4, where the fresh line's brain circled at 1.9 m/s); grass that pays only a slow nim takes it to about 95%.

**Limits.** One brain line and one world; two seeds for the reset at still 2, one for the control. The reset is a one-off edit of a checkpoint, not a floor kept during training, so the spread can collapse again; it shrinks slowly: thrust and turn 0.35 → 0.33 → 0.32 over 12M for the colony brain (g1f-c29-spread-more-s1, a second 6M at still 2: anchored 96.3%, stands median 36.7 s), turn 0.35 → 0.34 → 0.33 for the pond brain (p2t-more-s2, a second 6M at still 4: alive 161 · 149 s, pond speed halved to 0.56 · 0.57 m/s, long sits of 10 s or more fewer, 14 · 21 against 82 · 50). The confirm block 9,720,000 holds one seed of the reset-alone control and one of the plain colony run. The pond brain's line has the same problem on turning only (turn spread 0.03, thrust about 0.6), and resetting it ends the spinning that the turn cost couldn't (two seeds), and at the pond nearly doubles how long its nims live (two rungs, two seeds each): at still 4 by visiting the water far more often rather than moving slower, at still 1 at the same slow pond speed with far more long sits. The anchored measure counts spinning on the spot as anchored, so the spinner's 28.7% is not standing. Whether the colony brain keeps its colony skills after the reset isn't checked. The hunger term is on the grass-needs branch, not on next.
