# 0002 The body was the memory

date: 2026-11-05 · status: held-out · seeds: held-out 64 · brains: fs-parent, lam99-s2 (one family)

**Question.** With the tree up to ±12 m away and the first find free, a trained brain scored the same with its notes wiped. Where did the way back to the tree come from?

**World and brains.** The far tree (32 m arena, sight 4 m, 90 s games) with `[tree] start` 1.5: each game begins with the nims ringed 1.5 m off the trunk's edge, facing it. Two brains: the lineage's parent (`fs-parent`) and the same brain trained at GAE `lam` 0.99 (`lam99-s2`). Then `[tree] burrow`: an apple unloaded at home takes the carrier into the nest, and it comes back up at the nest's middle, turned any way. Frozen, no training here.

**Method.** 64 held-out games, apples home a minute, notes kept / wiped on unloading (wiping resets the notes every time it unloads at the nest). Baselines: picker, `searcher` and `tree_seen`.

**Result.** Apples home a minute:

| spread | picker | searcher | `tree_seen` | fs-parent | lam99-s2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ±6 m | 5.26 | 4.38 | 4.88 | 5.22 / 5.22 | 5.45 / 5.45 |
| ±9 m | 5.53 | 3.74 | 4.11 | 5.22 / 5.13 | 5.57 / 5.48 |
| ±12 m | 5.98 | 3.26 | 3.95 | 4.88 / 4.72 | 5.33 / 5.24 |
| ±12 m, burrow | 5.97 | 3.35 | 4.16 | 2.80 / 2.92 | 3.31 / 3.20 |

With the first find free, a script that remembers perfectly is about the picker, so the room for memory is picker less searcher: 0.9 a minute at ±6, 1.8 at ±9, 2.7 at ±12 (1.83 times). lam99-s2 came within 0.65 of the picker at ±12 m with notes worth nothing (5.33 kept, 5.24 wiped). In the burrow world the same brains fall to the searcher's level (lam99-s2 5.33 → 3.31) and the notes still don't matter in them (3.31, 3.20).

**What it rules out.** That the open-world score at ±12 m shows memory. The brain unloads at the nest's rim on the side it came in, so where it stands and which way it faces already point back at the tree. The way back was in the body, not the notes. The burrow closes that and leaves the 2.7 a minute between searcher and picker for memory to win.

**Limits.** Held-out seeds only. Two brains of one family, one pair of kept/wiped scores each, no intervals recorded in the notes for this table. Scored before the scorer fixes in #115, so not comparable game for game with the later findings. Not checked: whether the unloading position and facing, rather than something else the wipe takes, are the whole story in the open world.
