# 0006 Each remembers for itself

date: 2026-11-06 · status: confirmed · seeds: 8,105,000 · brains: duo2-s1, s2, s3 (one family, from mid2)

**Question.** With two Terrikins to a nest and one tree, does one use the other's notes?

**World and brains.** Rung C's world (no free first find: `[tree] start` off) with `[colonies] size = 2` and nothing else: two Terrikins, each with its own copy of the same brain and its own notes, sharing one nest and one tree. Untrained, company broke two of the three rung C brains (held-out 64, apples home a minute per nest: 2.78, 0.62 and 5.14, picker 8.77). Then 16M samples each in two chunks of 8M (`duo`, `duo2`).

**Method.** 256 fresh games from seed 8,105,000, scored three ways on the same games: notes kept, the one unloading wiped as it stores (self), both wiped. "Own notes" is kept less self; "nestmate's notes" is self less both. Picker 9.60, tree in sight 2.78, searcher 2.43 on the same games.

**Result.** Apples a minute per nest:

| brain | kept / self / both | own notes, kept − self | nestmate's notes, self − both |
|---|---|---|---|
| duo2-s1 | 9.54 / 8.72 / 8.73 | +0.82 [+0.64, +1.00] | −0.01 [−0.16, +0.14] |
| duo2-s2 | 8.56 / 7.18 / 7.18 | +1.38 [+1.04, +1.73] | +0.01 [−0.22, +0.22] |
| duo2-s3 | 7.72 / 6.41 / 6.59 | +1.32 [+0.90, +1.75] | −0.18 [−0.43, +0.03] |

After 16M the pairs reach 0.80–0.99 of the picker (s1 level with it). Both Terrikins deliver alike (e.g. 4.79 / 4.74).

**What it rules out.** That the notes carry across the nest: with one wiped, the other's intact notes don't help it. Each remembers for itself and nothing passes between them, as nothing in the world lets it.

**Limits.** Two to a nest only, one tree, one family. The Terrikins start young, so they eat from the store for free (see 0007 for what happens when eating has a price). With three to a nest at 48 m, a different world, the solo brain starts below the searcher (2.89 per nest against 3.89) and one of two runs there showed no memory gap (7.80 / 7.70), perhaps from following a nestmate out; untested.
