A meal from the store now counts for `[reward] needs`: it paid only apples, flowers and jelly from the ground, because eating from the store added to energy and jelly but not to what the reward counted as food. The world keeps a new `nourished` count of the energy a Terrikin actually kept from any meal (a young one's smaller bite and a meal that tops it up included), and `needs` pays on that, while `[reward] food` and the evals' `fed` still count food alone. Rewards with `needs = 0` are unchanged.
