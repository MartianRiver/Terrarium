A nim's body can be fuller: a fat reserve that food past a full bar goes into and that carries it through a night, resting and sleeping that cost less than walking, moving that costs by weight, what it carries and how fast it goes, stamina that comes back fastest asleep somewhere warm, nests that grow to hold their colony, and a record of what each nim died of (thirst, chilled, starved). All of it is off unless a world sets it, so every world plays as before; brains see their fat as one new sense (schema v23), and the budget prints what the body costs.
