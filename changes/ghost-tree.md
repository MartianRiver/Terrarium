A replay of a brain with memory can show where its notes put the tree: a faint see-through tree, tagged "where its notes put the tree", that fades in when a Terrikin comes up from unloading an apple and out when it has an apple again (or after 20 s). `python -m terrarium.ghost fit` reads the place off the notes of a frozen brain (a linear readout, per brain, about a metre off on mid2-s1), and `record --ghost` writes it into the replay as a new optional field, `ghosts`, so the format needs no new version and older viewers ignore it. In the moved-tree game the ghost stays at the old spot while the tree is somewhere else.
