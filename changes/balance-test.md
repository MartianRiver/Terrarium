`bench/balance.py` is the balance test for long worlds: scripted colonies (carrier, nester, or a forager that knows where the food patches and meadows are and gets home before dark) live for several days and nights in a world that never resets, the dead staying dead and hatchlings the only births, and it reports whether the world holds a population against rich and lean gates, at a supply set by food count or regrowth (`--supply`, `--by`), with `bench/balance_report.py` for the charts. In the two-colony world of nine the forager holds a rich supply (94% at the end of ten days, none die out) where carrier and nester can't, and nights are what bind.
