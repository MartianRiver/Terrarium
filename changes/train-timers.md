Every training round now says where its collecting went: `rays_seconds`, `rules_seconds`, `physics_seconds`, `brain_seconds` and `other_seconds` sit beside `collect_seconds` in each metrics line, so any run, on any machine, shows what its time was spent on. A queued training job can also ask for `fast = true` (compiled update in bfloat16) and `gpu_collect = true` (the brain picks for every world at once on the GPU), both off unless asked; a machine that can't use one drops it and says so, the run's `job.json` records what actually ran, and a worker older than version 14 won't take a job that asks. Nothing changes for jobs that don't ask. The coordinator needs deploying for it to hold back old workers.
