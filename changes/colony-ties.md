When two nims reach the same apple, flower or berry on one tick, or stand in two overlapping nests, the winner is now drawn at random from a seeded stream of its own for each game instead of always being the lowest index (which gave colony 0 about half a nim's lead); games stay reproducible from their seed, and `python -m terrarium.evals --seed N` plays any seed like `terrarium.games`, with the seed written into the `--out` JSON.
