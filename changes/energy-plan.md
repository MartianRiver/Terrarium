`python -m terrarium.budget WORLD.toml` prints a world's energy and water budget: what resting and walking cost a second by day and in the dark, how long a nim lasts on its food and its water, how far the food and the pond are from a nest (measured on the built world), what a trip there and home costs against what a full crop brings back, and the share of a day an adult spends keeping itself fed and watered, so a world's energy is set as a budget (trips about 5x their cost, a third of the day left) instead of one dial at a time. The roadmap says where the body gets more to it, stage by stage: an energy budget in 0.4, carrying paid by weight in 0.6, metabolism by body size and repletes in 0.8, fat in 0.9.
