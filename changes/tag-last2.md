Scored games that mix policies now tell a brain the move that was actually played last tick, not the one it proposed. In tag, when being "it" passes to or from a scripted nim (or another brain), the brain's last-thrust and last-turn senses were showing a move it never made on those ticks, and so did its notes, which advanced on the same input; training always showed the played move. Tag catch and escape scores against the chaser or a different brain can move (as can seek and flag scores against a scripted or different opponent); the same brain playing every side is unchanged bit for bit, and so are replays, training and the Catch them! game. Scores taken before this against mixed opponents were made with the old input, so rescoring is needed to compare across.
