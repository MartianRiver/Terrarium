A nim can get thirsty (`[water]`, schema v21): it has `water`, 1 full, that falls over time and fills while it stands in a pond, and at 0 it dies like starving. Ponds are flat discs set `distance` m from colony 0's nest in a random direction each game, with food and flowers kept out of them; rays say how far a pond's edge is (`pond`), as for a nest. Older brains load with zero weights for both new inputs, `[water] ponds = 0` leaves a world exactly as it was, replays carry the ponds and each nim's water, the viewer draws them and shows a water row in the vitals card, and `nursery-water.toml` is the tree nursery with one pond in sight of home and grown-up costs, so a nim gets thirsty within a game. Nothing rewards drinking yet.
