A world can be saved between two ticks and resumed later, on this machine or another, to carry on bit for bit (`terrarium.save`): every array, counter and random stream the world and its mechanics hold, the physics, the config and the commit, and a frozen brain's own state, notes included. Pymunk doesn't continue a rebuilt space exactly, so by default a save also settles the live world through the file, and the world and any resume of it agree from there on; `python -m terrarium.save --check WORLD.toml [BRAIN]` saves mid-run and compares every tick after. A two-colony world of nine saves to about 0.5 MB in under a tenth of a second.
