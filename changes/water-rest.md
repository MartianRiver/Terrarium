Water can drain slower while a nim keeps still and slower again while it sleeps: `[water] rest` and `asleep` are shares of `drain`, paid still (as energy's `rest`) and still in the dark and warm. Both are 1 in every world so far, so nothing changes until a world sets them. The budget prints how long water lasts still and asleep when they're set, and the ledger says how long a still nim lasts before thirst.
