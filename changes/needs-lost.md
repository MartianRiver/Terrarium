`[reward] needs_lost` charges the energy a nim loses to the drain, the cold and effort at the moment it goes, as the rise in hunger squared it makes (so 0 or less), where `needs` alone pays only a meal's fall and a night out in the cold cost nothing in reward short of death. With `needs` and `needs_lost` both 1 a tick pays the whole change in hunger squared. It pays nothing on a tick a nim dies or its arena ends, and not for one that hatches. Rewards with `needs_lost = 0` are unchanged; scratch train configs need the key, as with `needs`.
