A nim now dies when what it ate in a tick doesn't cover what it spent. Before, the energy was cut off at 0 before the meal went in and the unpaid part forgotten, so a nim grazing a cell that regrew less than the drain, with energy about 0, ended every tick a hair above 0 and never died. The same went for water (a sip smaller than the drain) and for the cost of a jump, a lift and a dirty nest; a meal or a drink that covers the debt still saves it on the tick it would have run out. This shifts the odds slightly in every world, since spending past empty used to be free.
`[ppo] bound` (off at 0) with `bound_for` (action names, every steering action when empty) adds a penalty on how far the policy's mean thrust and turn sit past ±1, where the world clips and nothing pulls the mean back; yes/no actions are left alone, and train configs without the keys still load.
`[reward] hunger` (off at 0, so every config trains as before) charges a living nim `hunger × (1 − energy)²` a second, and the same for thirst where there's water: hunger hurts all the time and fades near full. Not charged to the dead, the unborn or an arena about to start over.
