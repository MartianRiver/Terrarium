The ground can be food (`[grass]`, schema v22): cover in cells `cell` m across, full in `meadows` discs set `distance` m from colony 0's nest in a random direction each game (`base` elsewhere), that a nim whose energy is below 1 eats where it stands, by itself, `bite` cover a second for `food` energy a unit, and that grows back at `regrow` a second up to what each cell started with; with `still` set, grazing slows with speed to nothing at `still` m/s, as drinking does, so it takes standing still. A nim feels the cover under it and which way it rises (`grass`, `grass_sin`, `grass_cos`, last in the schema; older brains load with zero weights), worlds count what each has grazed and for how long, replays carry the grid in the header and the cover once a second (a byte a cell) with no new format version, and the viewer shows the lawn as bare ground with a soft green over it that thins as it's grazed and fills back. `nursery-grass.toml` is the water nursery's small world with grass as the only food, thirst, nights, the tree and jumping off, and two meadows by the nest, grazed at any speed. `budget.py` prints what grass gives. Worlds without `[grass]` meadows or base cover are exactly as they were.
