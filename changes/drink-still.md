Drinking can take standing still: with `[water] still` set, a nim in a pond drinks all of `drink` at a stop, less the faster it moves, and nothing at `still` m/s, so walking through a pond no longer counts as drinking. 0 (every world so far) leaves drinking as it was. Tried on the water nursery: at 0.5 m/s no nim ever slowed enough to drink and all died in 25 s; at 4 m/s (full speed drinks half) the thrust mean came down but stayed past the clip, so they drank slower rather than stopping.
