Thirst counts in the needs reward where a world has water: under `needs` a drink pays its fall in thirst squared, as a meal pays its fall in hunger squared, and `needs_lost` charges the water the drain takes as it goes. A nim now keeps a count of what it has drunk (`drank`), and a tick drains before it drinks, so a nim in a pond with its water nearly gone is counted the same as any other.
