`[night] start_dark` is a share of games, from 0 to 1, that start at nightfall instead of at dawn, drawn for each arena each time it starts over from the world's own random numbers. The nims start where they always do, at the nest, with no food standing yet, so a night is the whole game for them instead of one short stretch after a day of foraging. It is 0 in every world file, which plays exactly as before, and a recorded world from before it comes back with 0.
