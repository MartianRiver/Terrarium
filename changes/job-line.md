A queued job can say which line of work it belongs to and what it answers: `line = "nights"` and `question = "Does a dawn reward bring them home?"` in a batch file, at the top or on a job, for training and score jobs alike and for every piece of a chunked job. They are saved in the signed spec, so a run's `job.json` carries them (`spec.line`, `spec.question`) and the channel can group runs by line and show the question instead of reading a hand-written list. Jobs without them are unchanged, and no server needs deploying.
