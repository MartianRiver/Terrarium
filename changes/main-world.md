The main world, `sim/configs/main-world.toml`: two colonies of nine in 26 m with every skill the nurseries taught, in one world. It is the two-colony colony world with grass that feeds only a standing nim (two meadows near each colony, none growing or feeding in the dark), a pond that gives water only to a nim that stops in it (one, where neither nest is nearer), one apple tree in the middle (eight of the thirty foods hang on it, jumped for and carried home; the other twenty-two stay berries), rest and sleep that cost less than walking, sight to 12 m and a fat reserve. What it needed from the code: with several colonies the meadows are dealt out among them, each near its own nest, and a pond goes where no nest is more than a metre nearer than another, at its distance from the middle of the nests (with one colony both are placed exactly as before); `[grass] night` is how much of the grass there is in the dark, the share of its regrowth and of a bite it keeps (1 as before, 0 none); `[tree] apples` makes only that many foods hang on the tree and leaves the rest on the ground, where before a tree made every food an apple (0 as before, and it needs `home`, so an apple pays when it's unloaded and a berry never does); and a tree's pay goes to the colony that carried the apple home, where it went to everyone in the arena, which two colonies would have shared. Replays draw only the tree's own foods as apples.

The skills battery, `python -m terrarium.battery CKPT ... --seed S`: a frozen brain in each nursery skill's own world (stands to graze, drinks standing still, jumps for apples, goes back to the tree it remembers, stays home at night), one line a brain, so a skill that fades while the main world trains shows the same day. Its worlds are in `sim/configs/battery/`, and on the brains each finding named it reads what the finding did (0005's +18, 0031's 88%, 0030's 0.42 m/s).

`[tree] exists = true` keeps the tree and its apples whatever the tree pays, so `points = 0` no longer takes the tree away (left out, it is there when points is above 0, as before); the main world sets it. When a colony's store has less jelly than the hungry nims in its nest want, who gets a bite is drawn at random each tick from the arena's own tie stream, where it used to go to the lowest slot numbers first and starve the rest. A nim's last stretch before it dies or its game ends now pays its hunger and lost-energy costs like any other (it used to escape them), so dying hungry costs what it should in `needs_lost` and `hunger`. Wide rays now see a block, the tree's trunk included, anywhere in their slice of the fan, as they already saw berries and nims, where a small trunk 10 m off was seen at under a quarter of headings. The payoff ledger lists what the body costs a second (move, turn, rest, sleep, still, fat) beside the rewards, and its effort row now says that effort leaves out the drain. The balance test judges each colony as well as each arena, wants the population held every day, and calls a run under four days incomplete rather than a pass.
