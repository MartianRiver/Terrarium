A rented pod that's done (its worker ended or its hours ran out) now destroys itself on Vast instead of stopping, since a stopped Vast pod keeps billing for its disk.
The pod manager spends to a soft budget a week and a month instead of a hard cap a day: it rents while anything is left of today's share (what's left of the week over its days left), the week and the month, after what running pods will still cost, so the last pod can run over and the days after plan smaller; days start in Sydney (`week_usd`, `month_usd`, `zone` in pods.toml; `cap_usd_day` is gone). status.json adds the spend today, this week and this month, today's share and the room left.
